### Important facts to surface

- Statute of limitations is 5 years for credit cards and medical debt in Illinois
- Illinois wage garnishment is significantly more protective than federal (15% gross or 45x minimum wage, lesser)
- "Wild card" bank exemption protects up to $4,000 in a debtor's bank account but must be claimed in court
- 7-in-7 rule: collectors limited to seven calls in seven days, no contact within seven days of speaking to consumer
- Time-barred debt: making a small payment can restart the statute of limitations
- 2018 Illinois Supreme Court Rule 208.2 requires debt buyers to attach evidence when filing collection lawsuits

### Utility shutoff protections (overlaps with housing)

See full utility section under Housing. Key facts:
- Winter moratorium under ICC rules: no shutoff December 1 to March 31 for qualifying households who apply for LIHEAP, have a serious illness, or meet other criteria
- 60-day deferral of initial credit and deposit for domestic violence victims under Public Utilities Act (220 ILCS 5/8-201.6)
- PIPP enrolls eligible households in budget billing

### Important 2026 changes to flag

**SNAP work requirements (effective Feb 1, 2026)**
- Most recipients ages 18 to 64 must report at least 80 work hours per month
- Exceptions for dependent children, disability, and other categories
- ABAWDs limited to 3 months of benefits in a 3-year period if not meeting work requirements

**Medicaid changes from One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA)**
- Work requirements (80 hours/month) for expansion adults age 19 to 64 start 2027
- Renewals shift from annual to every 6 months starting December 2026
- Up to $35 per specialist visit out-of-pocket starting October 2028
- Primary care and preventive services remain free

### 2026 income thresholds (selected)

- Federal Poverty Level for 1 person: ~$1,304/month
- Medicaid expansion adults: up to 138% FPL
- All Kids (children, no immigration restrictions): up to 318% FPL, no asset test
- Pregnant women: up to 213% FPL with presumptive eligibility
- SNAP gross income: up to 165% FPL (200% FPL for elderly/disabled households)
- LIHEAP: up to 60% State Median Income

### Special notes on immigration and benefits

- Children under 18 can apply for All Kids regardless of immigration status
- Undocumented adults 65+ qualify for state-funded Medicaid in Illinois
- Pregnant people qualify for prenatal Medicaid regardless of immigration status (Moms & Babies)
- Most lawful permanent residents qualify for full Medicaid after 5 years
- Emergency Medicaid covers life-threatening emergencies for everyone

## Suggestions for the project

Some patterns showed up across this research:

**Demand exceeds supply for almost everything.** Home repair programs have multi-year waitlists. LIHEAP funds run out before August. ERAP intake closes for stretches. The chatbot should manage expectations clearly: "this program exists, but funds may be exhausted, here is how to get on the waitlist anyway."

**Eligibility depends heavily on geography.** Chicago vs suburban Cook vs collar counties each have different programs. The chatbot probably needs to ask zip code early in any housing or repair conversation.

**Immigration status matters but does not always disqualify.** Many programs (CCLAHD housing aid, All Kids, prenatal Medicaid, RAP rental help, MTO services) are explicitly open regardless of status. Some (SNAP, full Medicaid for adults) have restrictions. The chatbot should not assume disqualification.

**The Safer Homes Act disclosure rule (effective Jan 1, 2026) is new.** Most landlords are required to provide a 4-page summary of survivor rights with every lease. Tenants who did not get this summary may have a damages claim.

**The Illinois Collection Agency Act was almost repealed but updated by SB 2457 effective Jan 1, 2026.** It no longer sunsets. This is fresh content most generic legal explainers may not have yet.

**Statute citations matter.** Each answer should ideally include the exact citation (765 ILCS 705/, 735 ILCS 5/12-803, etc.) so a downstream legal aid attorney can verify quickly and so the chatbot's authority is legible.
