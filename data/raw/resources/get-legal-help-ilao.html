Get Legal Help

Get legal help is a tool to guide you to the best available service or resource. To give you the best referrals, we will ask you questions about your legal problem, your location or the location of your legal problem, and your income.

If you are looking for help from a lawyer, you will answer questions about your issue first. Then, if available, you will complete an online application.

Enter your problem and pick the best match from the list. If you don't see a match, keep going.
Enter your zip code or the zip code of your legal problem.
What type of help are you looking for?
Select all that apply to your situation.
For household, count yourself and these people if they live with you: (1) your spouse unless you are asking for legal help against them; (2) kids under 18; and (3) other adults not paying rent or food.
Please answer household size first.
Estimate income before taxes are taken out
Do any of the following describe you? Check all that apply.
Organizations are not able to help everyone that applies. Some organizations offer assistance to special populations and not to others.
