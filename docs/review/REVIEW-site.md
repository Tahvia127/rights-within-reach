# Site Reviewer Guide — Rights Within Reach

Thank you for reviewing the site! You're giving it a fresh pair of eyes as a
**user and a tester**. You don't need any technical or legal background — we want
to know if the site is clear, trustworthy, easy to use, and works everywhere.

Site: **https://www.rightswithinreach.org**

Please log everything you find in the **Issue Log** at the bottom (copy the table
into a doc/sheet, or email us the rows).

---

## 1. First impressions (5 minutes)
Open the home page and, without clicking much, answer:
- [ ] Is it clear what this site is and who it's for, within a few seconds?
- [ ] Does it feel trustworthy and calm (not spammy, not scary)?
- [ ] Do you notice the disclaimer that this is information, not legal advice?
- [ ] Is the text big enough and easy to read?

## 2. Navigation & structure
- [ ] Can you get to all four topics (Housing, Money, Repairs, Benefits)?
- [ ] Can you find the Resources page and the "Ask a question" chat?
- [ ] Do the top menu, bottom menu, and footer links all work?
- [ ] Does "Back" / the logo always get you home?

## 3. Content clarity & accuracy
For each topic page and the Resources page:
- [ ] Is the language plain and easy to understand?
- [ ] Does anything sound like legal *advice* rather than information? (flag it)
- [ ] Do phone numbers, hours, and addresses on org cards look correct/current?
- [ ] Are there typos, broken sentences, or confusing parts?
- [ ] Do outbound links go to the right place?

## 4. The chat / "Ask a question" flow
- [ ] Try the guided steps (where you live → topic → your question).
- [ ] Try skipping the guided steps and just asking.
- [ ] Ask a normal question (e.g., "My landlord won't fix the heat — what can I do?")
      — is the answer clear, with next steps and who to contact?
- [ ] Ask something **out of scope** (e.g., a criminal or immigration question)
      — does it politely decline and point you somewhere instead of guessing?
- [ ] Do the suggested follow-up questions make sense?
- [ ] Does the disclaimer appear with answers?

## 5. Languages
Switch languages with the bar at the top and spot-check each page.
- [ ] English [ ] Español [ ] 中文 [ ] Tagalog [ ] Tiếng Việt
- [ ] Does the **whole** page switch (nothing stuck in English)?
- [ ] Does your selected language stick as you move between pages?
- (Deep wording review is handled separately by language reviewers — here, just
  flag anything obviously still in English or visibly broken.)

## 6. Read-aloud (listen) feature
- [ ] Find a "Read aloud" / "Listen" button and play it.
- [ ] Open the **voice & speed** control (top bar) — change the speed slider and
      the voice. Does playback follow your choice?
- [ ] Does starting one section stop the previous one?
- [ ] Try it in another language — does it read in that language (or tell you no
      voice is installed)?

## 7. Accessibility
- [ ] Use the **larger-text** toggle — does the layout still work?
- [ ] Navigate using only the **Tab key** — can you reach every button/link, and
      can you see which one is focused?
- [ ] If you use a screen reader, do buttons/images announce sensibly?
- [ ] Is text easy to read against its background (contrast)?

## 8. Devices & browsers
Try the site on as many as you can and note any layout breakage:
- [ ] Phone (iPhone Safari and/or Android Chrome)
- [ ] Tablet
- [ ] Desktop: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge
- [ ] Rotate your phone (portrait/landscape)

## 9. Speed & errors
- [ ] Do pages load reasonably fast?
- [ ] Does anything error out, spin forever, or show a blank screen?
- [ ] If the chat fails, is there a friendly message (not a raw error)?
