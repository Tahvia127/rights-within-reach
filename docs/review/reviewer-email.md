# Reviewer outreach email

Copy-paste and send one per reviewer. Replace the **[bracketed]** parts and attach
that reviewer's CSV: `es.csv` (Spanish), `zh.csv` (Chinese), `tl.csv` (Tagalog), or
`vi.csv` (Vietnamese) from `docs/review/translations/`.

---

**Subject:** Can you review the [LANGUAGE] translations for Rights Within Reach? (~1–2 hrs)

Hi [NAME],

Thank you so much for agreeing to help! **Rights Within Reach** is a free website that
gives Illinois residents plain-language legal information about housing, money and debt,
home repairs, and public benefits — in five languages. Every non-English translation was
drafted by machine, so before we share it publicly we need a native speaker to make sure
it's accurate, natural, and respectful. That's where you come in — you don't need any
legal or technical background, just fluency.

**What I'm asking**

I've attached a spreadsheet with every piece of [LANGUAGE] text on the site. Please open
it in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers. Each row has these columns:

- **English (source)** — the original meaning (this is the source of truth)
- **[LANGUAGE]** — the draft to review
- **status (OK / FIX)** — type `OK` if it's fine, `FIX` if it needs changing
- **suggested correction** — if you wrote `FIX`, put your better version here
- **notes** — anything you'd like to flag

Rows marked **`MISSING`** have no translation yet — please add one in the
"suggested correction" column. You can ignore the "key" column.

**What to look for**

- **Meaning matches the English** — this matters most.
- **Legal terms are the ones people and local agencies actually use** (eviction, lease,
  benefits, notice, deposit…), not literal dictionary words.
- **Tone is calm and respectful** — many readers are stressed (facing eviction, debt, etc.).
- **Plain and simple** — everyday language a busy person can read quickly.
- **Numbers, dollar amounts, dates, and law citations stay exactly as the English**
  (e.g., "$1,000", "30 days", "225 ILCS 425").
- **Nothing important is left in English** by accident (our name "Rights Within Reach"
  and organization names can stay as-is).

**Optional, but really helpful:** if you have a few extra minutes, visit
**https://www.rightswithinreach.org**, switch to [LANGUAGE] using the bar at the top, and
skim the main pages (Housing, Money & Debt, Home Repairs, Public Benefits, Resources).
Note anything that reads oddly — those longer pages aren't in the spreadsheet.

**Sending it back**

Just save the file and reply to this email with it attached. If the same word is wrong in
many places, tell me once and I'll fix every instance. Could you get it back to me by
**[DATE]**? It should take about 1–2 hours.

Thank you again — your review is what makes this safe and trustworthy for the community.

Warmly,
[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR EMAIL / PHONE]
